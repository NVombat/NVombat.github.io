About Me

I did my undergrad studies in Computer Science and Engineering with a specialisation in Cyber Security from SRM Institute of Science & Technology in Chennai, India. I have a Master's degree in Computer Science from the University of Calgary, Canada where I worked in the area of Access Control, specifically Zero Trust Architectures, Policy Mining and Automated Policy Formulation under the supervision of Prof. Philip W.L. Fong.

I am passionate about software development, particularly backend systems, and enjoy building reliable, scalable solutions. At the same time, I remain deeply curious about cybersecurity and the broader challenges of designing systems that are both functional and resilient. I am especially drawn to hands-on roles that allow me to continuously learn, experiment, and grow within the ever-evolving landscape of computer science.

Outside of work, I thrive on staying active and exploring new challenges. I have a strong interest in skiing and am eager to pursue surfing and snowboarding. I play football, squash, badminton, basketball, tennis, table tennis, cricket, and snooker, and also enjoy running, cycling, hiking, and camping.

Creativity is another important part of my life. I enjoy acting, playing the tabla, guitar, and ukulele, and I love to sing and write rap. I have released a couple of songs on streaming platforms—No Oxygen and Lovers On The Run—and I look forward to creating and producing more music in the future.

Coming from a military background, I have had the opportunity to live in different parts of India, which has shaped my adaptability and perspective. Recently, I've developed a strong interest in traveling and hope to continue exploring new places, cultures, and experiences around the world.
