How the Game Works

Make Your Picks

Select 8 teams and predict how far each will go:

  • 2 Round of 32 picks

Score Points

Each correct prediction earns you points:

Round of 32 1 pt
Round of 16 3 pts
Quarter-final 6 pts
Semi-final 10 pts
Final 15 pts
Winner 22 pts

Key Rules

  • Each team can only be picked once
  • No duplicate teams in your entry
  • If a team reaches at least your predicted stage, you score!
  • Max score: 61 points

Winning

  • The leaderboard will display all users ranked by points (highest points first)
  • The user with the most points wins
  • Tie-breaking: Equal scores are ordered by earliest submission time
  • Check the leaderboard after the submission deadline to see rankings
