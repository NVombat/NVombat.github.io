About Me

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Calgary, specializing in Zero-Trust Architectures & Policy Mining. With a strong academic record (CGPA 3.93/4.00) and multiple prestigious scholarships including the MITACS Globalink Graduate Fellowship and Alberta Innovates Graduate Student Scholarship, I am deeply committed to advancing cybersecurity research.

My research focuses on developing innovative solutions in zero-trust security architectures, policy mining, and automated policy formulation. I have published several papers in top-tier conferences and journals, including work on authorship attribution methods and malware analysis frameworks.

Beyond research, I serve as a Graduate Student Representative on multiple committees at the University of Calgary, including the Faculty of Science Awards Committee and Info-Sec Hiring Committees. I also contribute to academia through various teaching assistant positions in courses ranging from Access Control to Data Structures and Operating Systems.

My technical expertise spans across cybersecurity, software development, and cloud technologies. I have experience in developing scalable applications using Django, FASTAPI, and Python, and have worked on projects involving cyber attribution systems and zero-trust access control models.
